# Fluent — Distribution & Go-to-Market

## Stage

Pre-revenue, pre-launch. Currently in active development (Phase 2 of 5 complete).
First target: get the product working end-to-end, then validate with real users from the stuttering community.

## Primary Channel: Community-Led GTM

The stuttering community is tight-knit, digitally active, and deeply underserved. They trust each other — not ads.

### Tier 1 — Online Communities

| Community | Platform | Approach |
|-----------|----------|----------|
| r/Stutter | Reddit | Founder posts personal story + product demo. Community does the spreading. |
| Stuttering Foundation | Forums/email | Reach out to leadership. Educational angle: "tool for the community" |
| National Stuttering Association (NSA) | US org | Conference presence, newsletter placement, credibility anchor |
| StutterTalk podcast | Podcast | Guest appearance — Noam's story as person who stutters building this |
| YouTube stutter community | Videos | Seed with authentic demo. "How I made a phone call without stuttering" |

### Tier 2 — Founder Story (LinkedIn + X)

- Noam shares his personal stuttering experience
- Documents building Fluent publicly ("building in public")
- Posts outcomes: "I just booked my first dentist appointment using my own product"
- Authentic, emotional, highly shareable in the disability / neurodiversity space
- LinkedIn for professional credibility; X for reach and virality

### Tier 3 — Product Hunt (at launch)

- Launch when Phase 3+ is complete (real-time transcript + barge-in)
- "Built for people who stutter" is a compelling Product Hunt story
- Aim for Top 5 in day — stuttering as a hook is rare and emotionally resonant

## Acquisition Funnel

```
Awareness: Community post / founder story / YouTube demo
    ↓
Landing page: clear value prop, "Try 1 call free"
    ↓
Sign up: email only, no credit card
    ↓
Onboarding: record voice (10 min) → first call
    ↓
AHA moment: hear the call go through in your own voice
    ↓
Activation: second call within 7 days
    ↓
Monetization: hit free-tier limit → upgrade to Premium
```

## Retention Levers

- **Call history as a record** — seeing all the calls you've made builds attachment
- **Outcome summaries** — tangible results increase perceived value
- **Voice clone ownership** — users feel emotionally attached to their voice profile
- **Calendar sync (Phase 5)** — calls become part of their workflow

## Pricing Strategy

- **Free plan exists** — low barrier to first call, proves the product
- **$19/mo Premium** — the "serious user" tier, priced for individuals not businesses
- **$49/mo Pro** — unlimited; for high-frequency users (small business owners who stutter, therapists, etc.)
- **No annual plan in v1** — simplify billing, validate monthly first

## What We Are NOT Doing (v1 GTM)

- No paid ads (audience is small and specific — community-led is more efficient)
- No SEO content farming (not enough search volume for "tools for stuttering" yet)
- No B2B sales (Fluent is a personal product, not an enterprise solution)
- No influencer marketing (stuttering creators may not want to be pigeonholed as "stutter tool promoters")
- No affiliate program (too early, adds billing complexity)

## Key Metrics to Track

| Metric | Why It Matters |
|--------|----------------|
| Activation rate (completed voice clone) | Measures onboarding friction |
| First call completion rate | Core product validation |
| 7-day return rate | Does the product stick? |
| Free → Premium conversion | Monetization signal |
| Calls per active user per month | Engagement depth |
| Churn (month-over-month) | Retention health |

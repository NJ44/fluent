# Fluent — The Offer

## What You're Selling

**Fluent is a personal AI phone agent that calls on your behalf, in your cloned voice.**

The user buys:
1. **Your voice on the phone** — ElevenLabs-cloned, sounds like you (not a robot)
2. **The call made for you** — AI handles it, you watch the transcript
3. **Control when you want it** — one button to jump in (barge-in)
4. **A record of what happened** — outcome summary + full transcript
5. **Freedom from the dread** — the phone is no longer a problem

**Billing:** PayPal subscriptions (not Stripe — global compatibility)

### Free Plan Logic
- 1 call per calendar month
- Enough to validate the product; not enough to rely on it
- No credit card required to start
- Designed to be genuinely useful (one appointment per month is realistic)
- Converts when the user hits the limit during a month they needed Fluent again

### Premium Plan Logic ($19/mo)
- 10 calls per month — covers the average working adult's call needs
- Priced below "obvious skip": $19 is less than one missed appointment rescheduled
- Target: adults with routine call needs (doctors, appointments, customer service)

### Pro Plan Logic ($49/mo)
- Unlimited — for power users (small business owners, professionals with high call volume who stutter)
- Also the right tier for users who need voice re-cloning flexibility and maximum control

## What's Always Included (All Plans)
- One-time voice clone (60–120s recording → ElevenLabs IVC)
- Voice re-clone at any time from Settings
- Consent and privacy controls (voice is private, deletable)
- TCPA-compliant AI disclosure on every call
- What-if fallback rules (2–3 per call)
- Live transcript visible during call
- Barge-in button (one press: your mic goes live, AI goes silent)
- 1-sentence outcome summary after every call
- Full transcript stored and viewable
- Call history with intent + outcome + date

## What Comes in Phase 5 (Post-MVP)
- Google Calendar push from post-call screen
- Call retry with same or edited intent
- Usage quota display on dashboard
- Upgrade prompt when limit hit (not error)
- PayPal subscription management (upgrade / downgrade)

## Emotional Pitch

> "You've been putting off that call for two weeks. Type what you need. Hit Send. Done."

The offer isn't "AI phone agent." The offer is **freedom from the calls you've been avoiding.**

## Competitive Framing

| What they compare it to | Why Fluent wins |
|------------------------|-----------------|
| Asking a friend/spouse to call | Private. Not humiliating. Available at 11pm. |
| Email/text instead | Some things only work via phone (doctor offices, most service businesses) |
| Speech therapy | Takes months. Helps in practice, not always in real calls. Fluent works right now. |
| Other AI assistants (generic) | Generic AI voice ≠ your voice. Fluent sounds like you. |
| Just muscling through the call | That works sometimes. Fluent removes the dread entirely. |

## Onboarding Flow (What the User Experiences on Day 1)

1. Sign up with email
2. Record 60–120s voice sample (browser mic, scripted passage)
3. Audio quality check passes
4. Record consent passphrase (identity + permission)
5. Hear cloned voice play back → confirm
6. Land on Dashboard
7. Type first intent → add phone number → hit Send
8. Watch transcript live
9. Read outcome summary

Total time from signup to first call: ~10 minutes.

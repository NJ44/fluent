# Fluent — Mission & Vision

## Mission

Give people who stutter full access to phone calls — on their own terms, in their own voice.

## Problem

~80 million people worldwide stutter (roughly 1% of the global population). For many, making phone calls is the most anxiety-inducing daily task they face. Not because they can't communicate — they absolutely can — but because the telephone format punishes hesitation:
- The other person doesn't know what's happening and grows impatient
- There's no way to pause, type, or recover with dignity
- Every call risks humiliation in front of strangers: receptionists, doctors, restaurants, customer service reps
- The fear compounds: "What if I block on my name?" "What if I can't say the number?"

The result: people who stutter routinely **avoid making calls they need to make** — postponing appointments, skipping reservations, tolerating problems that a 30-second call would fix. This isn't a communication impairment. It's a structural mismatch between the phone medium and the way they speak.

## Solution

Fluent is a **Personal Vocal Proxy**. The user:
1. Types what they need done (a goal in plain English)
2. Hits Send
3. An AI agent — speaking in the user's own cloned voice — makes the call for them
4. The user watches the live transcript on their screen and can barge in at any moment with one button press

After the call: a one-sentence outcome summary. Optionally pushed to calendar.

## Core Value Proposition

> **The AI makes the call the second you hit Send — in your own voice, without ever stuttering.**

Key components of that promise:
- **Your voice** — not a robot voice, not a generic TTS. Your actual voice, cloned. The person on the other end hears *you*, just fluent.
- **The second you hit Send** — no scheduling, no delay. Instant outbound.
- **Without ever stuttering** — the emotional release is the product. Not "AI does stuff" but "you never have to dread this again."

## Why This Matters

Phone anxiety driven by stuttering is not a niche edge case. 80M people globally. Adults who stutter report:
- Avoiding job calls, doctor calls, business calls
- Using email as a proxy when a call would be faster
- Asking friends or family to make calls on their behalf (dependency and embarrassment)
- Turning down opportunities that required phone presence

Fluent doesn't fix stuttering. It gives people their calls back.

## What Fluent Is NOT

- Not a therapy tool or fluency trainer
- Not a transcription service
- Not a voice assistant (no inbound, no wake word)
- Not an enterprise product — solo user, personal use
- Not Boltcall — Boltcall is B2B speed-to-lead for businesses. Fluent is B2C empowerment for individuals.

## Founder Angle

Noam (founder, 23, Israel) has personal experience with stuttering. This is not a "problem I found in a market report." It is lived experience. That emotional authenticity is the go-to-market story.
